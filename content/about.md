The Indonesian Civilians Cyber Watch Dog Association is an organization focused on problems
in the digital world effecting the Indonesian people.

## What are we?
We are a group of people who like computers and the digital world in general. We believe digital rights are one of the new bases of human rights.

## Why are we here?
We have seen digital rights erosion being massively charged up globally, de-anonymization laws sped up globally, surveillance in the name of children, big tech collecting more data than it should have, wrongful arrest just because you say something bad in the eyes of the government, etc. While we realize there are already tons of digital rights organizations worldwide, most of their actions are reactive. Not proactive or preventive. We hope we can fill the gap.

## What is our goal?
Our current goal is simple: making sure the digital realm is a safe and convenient place for everyone, without fear. Even if your opinion is viewed as bad by most people, you still feel safe expressing it, without fear of being prosecuted or psychologically scarred just because you said "not so good thing". Humans make mistakes; that's how they improve.

## What is our mission?
Fight for digital rights, that's it. For now, we focus on the most basic human rights being violated: privacy and freedom of expression. We might also join other campaigns, who knows ;)
